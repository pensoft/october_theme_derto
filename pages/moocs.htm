The MOOCs (Massive Open Online Courses) are hosted by the Society for Ecological Restoration on the SER Learn platform and offer structured learning on topics related to ecosystem restoration, including techniques, principles, and project management.

The first course, Basics in Ecological Restoration (ER), will provide an introduction to ER. The second course, Advanced Methods in Ecological Restoration, will cover more advanced methods for restoring ecosystems. Both courses are currently in development and are anticipated to be live in May 2026.

Upon successful completion of each MOOC, learners will have the opportunity to purchase a certificate, provided by this globally-renowned ER entity.
